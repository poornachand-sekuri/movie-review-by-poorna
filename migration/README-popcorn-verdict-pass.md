# Human-tone editorial pass

## Popcorn Verdict rules
- Read the full review body before writing the verdict.
- Keep it crisp enough for both home and review pages.
- Use simple, everyday language.
- Preserve the author's natural casual, sarcastic, emotional and playful tone.
- Make it sound like something the author would naturally type after watching the movie.
- Verdict must stand alone and make complete sense.
- Do not merely copy or truncate lines from the review.
- Do not use double dashes.
- Avoid polished critic-speak, formal review language, generic AI summaries, repetitive templates and over-neat sentence structures.

## Review body rules
- Use the original archived WordPress post as the main voice reference.
- Preserve the author's actual opinions, jokes, CAPS, emojis, Telugu phrases, personal stories and natural imperfections.
- Remove formulaic or over-polished AI-style phrasing introduced during migration.
- Prefer light cleanup over rewriting.
- Remove migration/social-post clutter only where needed for the website, such as a leading `Watched #Movie:` label.
- Do not make the prose more formal than the original.
- Do not use double dashes.
